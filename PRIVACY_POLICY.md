# Privacy Policy for Smart Shopping List

**Last updated: October 26, 2023**

This Privacy Policy describes how Smart Shopping List ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application (the "Service").

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**We do not collect any personal data.**

The Smart Shopping List app is designed to store your shopping list data locally on your device using your device's internal storage. We do not have access to this data, and it is never transmitted to our servers.

## 2. Local Storage

This app uses local storage technologies (specifically, Shared Preferences or a local database) to save your shopping list items directly on your device. This ensures that your list persists between app sessions. You have full control over this data and can delete items or clear the app's data through your device settings at any time.

## 3. Advertising (AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display banner advertisements in our application. AdMob is an advertising service provided by Google Inc.

Google AdMob may use cookies and other tracking technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our app or other apps on the internet. These technologies allow Google and its partners to serve personalized ads to you.

For more information on how Google uses the collected information, please visit the "How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps" page: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 4. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
