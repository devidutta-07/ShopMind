# SMART SHOPPING LIST - PHASE 0 - COMPLETION INSTRUCTIONS

Congratulations! Your "Smart Shopping List" app code is ready. This Phase 0 build focuses on simplicity and core functionality.

Here are the steps to finalize your app, generate the necessary files, and submit it to the Google Play Store.

## 1. UPDATE APP ICON
You need to generate the app icon files. The code expects the default Flutter launcher icon configuration, but for a polished look:

1.  Prepare your source image: A 1024x1024 PNG of a shopping cart with a checkmark (using your color palette: #8DBF42, #FF4A4A, #4A90E2, #FFC857).
2.  Go to [appicon.co](https://appicon.co/) or use Android Studio's "Image Asset Studio" (Right-click `android/app/src/main/res` -> New -> Image Asset).
3.  Replace the contents of `android/app/src/main/res/mipmap-*` folders with your generated icons.

## 2. CONFIGURE ADMOB (IMPORTANT)
The app is currently using **TEST IDs**. You must replace them with your real AdMob IDs before releasing.

1.  **App ID:**
    -   Open `android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml`.
    -   Find the `<meta-data android:name="com.google.android.gms.ads.APPLICATION_ID" ... />` tag.
    -   Replace `ca-app-pub-3940256099942544~3347511713` with your **REAL AdMob App ID**.

2.  **Banner Ad Unit ID:**
    -   Open `lib/screens/home_screen.dart`.
    -   Find `final String _adUnitId = 'ca-app-pub-3940256099942544/6300978111';`.
    -   Replace the string with your **REAL AdMob Banner Ad Unit ID**.

## 3. GENERATE SIGNED APK & AAB
To distribute your app, you need to sign it.

1.  **Create a Keystore:**
    -   Open a terminal in the project root.
    -   Run: `keytool -genkey -v -keystore upload-keystore.jks -keyalg RSA -keysize 2048 -validity 10000 -alias upload`
    -   Follow the prompts (set a password and remember it!).
    -   Move the generated `upload-keystore.jks` file to the `android/app` directory.

2.  **Configure Gradle:**
    -   Open `android/key.properties` (create it if it doesn't exist) and add:
        ```
        storePassword=<your-store-password>
        keyPassword=<your-key-password>
        keyAlias=upload
        storeFile=upload-keystore.jks
        ```
    -   Open `android/app/build.gradle`. Ensure the `signingConfigs` block reads from `key.properties` (standard Flutter setup usually handles this, or you can manually configure it).

3.  **Build the App Bundle (AAB) for Play Store:**
    -   Run: `flutter build appbundle`
    -   Output: `build/app/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab` -> **UPLOAD THIS TO PLAY STORE**

4.  **Build the APK (for direct installation/testing):**
    -   Run: `flutter build apk`
    -   Output: `build/app/outputs/flutter-apk/app-release.apk`

## 4. PLAY STORE ASSETS
You need these for your store listing.

*   **App Name:** Smart Shopping List
*   **Short Description:** Simple, clean shopping list with local storage. No login required.
*   **Full Description:**
    Smart Shopping List is the easiest way to organize your grocery shopping.

    **Features:**
    *   **Simple & Clean:** Minimalist design with a fresh color palette.

    *   **Ad-Supported:** Supported by a single, non-intrusive banner ad.
    *   **Privacy First:** We do not collect any personal data.

    Create, edit, and check off items with ease. Never forget an item again!

*   **Graphics:**
    *   **Icon:** 512x512 PNG (shopping cart with checkmark).
    *   **Feature Graphic:** 1024x500 PNG (use the apple green #8DBF42 background with the app name or icon).
    *   **Screenshots:** Take at least 4 screenshots of the app (Home Screen with items, Empty Home Screen, Add Item Screen).

## 5. PRIVACY POLICY
The Play Store requires a privacy policy URL.
1.  Open the `PRIVACY_POLICY.md` file in this project.
2.  Copy the HTML section at the bottom.
3.  Host it on a free site (like Google Sites, GitHub Pages, or a dedicated privacy policy host).
4.  Paste that URL into the "App Content" -> "Privacy Policy" section in the Google Play Console.

## 6. SUBMISSION
1.  Go to the [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console).
2.  Create a new app ("Smart Shopping List").
3.  Upload your **AAB** file to the "Production" or "Open Testing" track.
4.  Fill in the store listing details (Title, Descriptions, Graphics).
5.  Complete the "App Content" section (Privacy Policy, Ads, App Access, Content Ratings, Data Safety).
    *   **Data Safety:** Declare that you collect "Device or other IDs" for "App functionality" and "Advertising or marketing" (standard for AdMob).
6.  Submit for review!

Good luck with your Phase 0 launch!
